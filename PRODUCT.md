# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leaders, founders, and potential collaborators evaluating a software engineer. They should quickly understand the engineer's range, judgment, and experience while enjoying an unusually memorable portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Present Muhammad Reebal Raza’s factual, comprehensive professional profile through a calm, minimal single-page portfolio. The site should leave visitors believing this engineer can turn difficult systems into thoughtful, dependable products, then invite them to make contact.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and quietly confident. A minimal typographic language with disciplined type, one restrained accent, and day and night themes.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark developer portfolios, fake terminal screens, violet-to-blue gradients, neon glow, repeated card grids, ornamental dashboard metrics, and scroll-driven spectacle.

## Design Principles

- Let the content lead: typography and space do the work, with no scroll choreography.
- Keep one accent: color is rare so it reads as signal.
- Treat day and night as equals: both themes get the same contrast and care.
- End with confidence: the final contact action should resolve and hold, not trail into a generic footer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, complete keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, minimum 44px touch targets, and a reduced-motion experience that keeps all content.
