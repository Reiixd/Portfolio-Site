# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leaders, founders, and potential collaborators evaluating a software engineer. They should quickly understand the engineer's range, judgment, and experience while enjoying an unusually memorable portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Present Muhammad Reebal Raza’s factual, comprehensive professional profile through an interactive, scroll-driven portfolio. The site should leave visitors believing this engineer can turn difficult systems into thoughtful, dependable products, then invite them to make contact.

## Brand Personality

Technical, intense, and precise. The atmosphere takes cues from Cyberpunk 2077, reduced to a premium-minimal visual language with disciplined typography, focused color, and purposeful motion.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark developer portfolios, fake terminal screens, violet-to-blue gradients, neon glow overload, repeated card grids, and ornamental dashboard metrics. The site must not feel like a game HUD pasted over a résumé.

## Design Principles

- Make progress feel like discovery: every scroll movement should reveal a new layer of the engineer's story.
- Build one continuous world: transitions should feel spatial and connected, never like stacked templates.
- Let experience earn the peak: the career narrative receives the most visual transformation and scroll room.
- Keep cyberpunk disciplined: use tension, contrast, and signal color without visual noise.
- End with confidence: the final contact action should resolve and hold, not trail into a generic footer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, complete keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, minimum 44px touch targets, and a reduced-motion experience that preserves the full narrative without spatial travel.
