## Subject

## Art direction

A deliberately illustrated cyber-nocturne world: crisp graphic character art, deep blue-black architecture, controlled acid-yellow signals, and pale interface ink. It should feel like a single digital workspace that expands into a career city as the visitor travels.

## Journey

1. Arrival: meet the engineer at work and understand the promise.
2. Capability: see disciplines emerge from the tools around the desk.
3. Proof: enter selected projects as working system artifacts.
4. Memory: experience the career constellation assemble into one connected story.
5. Perspective: learn the principles behind the work.
6. Contact: arrive at a quiet communications terminal and make contact.

## Feeling curve

- Arrival: intrigue, caused by a close, inhabited workspace that reacts immediately to scroll.
- Capability: acceleration, caused by the workspace opening into layered technical systems.
- Proof: confidence, caused by concrete product stories and outcomes replacing abstraction.
- Memory: awe, caused by every prior signal locking into a luminous career constellation.
- Perspective: clarity, caused by motion settling while concise engineering principles remain.
- Contact: resolve, caused by the world arriving at one calm, unmistakable action.
